**Hop-In** is a comprehensive hostel management platform that connects students with quality hostel accommodations. The platform streamlines the entire hostel booking process, from discovery to move-in, with dedicated features for both hostel owners and students.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎓 For Students
* **Discovery:** Browse and search hostels with detailed listings and filters.
* **Visuals:** View high-quality images and a comprehensive list of amenities.
* **Real-time Status:** Check room availability instantly.
* **Bookings:** Secure online booking system and a "save for later" wishlist.
* **Feedback:** Read community reviews and write your own experiences.

### 💼 For Hostel Owners
* **Property Control:** Easy hostel registration and room/bed management.
* **Live Tracking:** Real-time booking and occupancy tracking.
* **Operations:** Integrated payment processing and student management system.
* **Insights:** Performance analytics to track growth and revenue.
